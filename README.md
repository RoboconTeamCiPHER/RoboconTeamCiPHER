# Team CiPHER 🤖

**National-Level Robotics Team | JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering, Pune**

Team CiPHER is a student-led robotics team focused on **designing, building, programming, and testing robotic systems** for competitions, research, and real-world applications.

We bring together multidisciplinary engineering across **Mechanical Design, Electronics, Programming, and Administration** to transform ideas into working robotic systems.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to develop engineers who can solve complex robotics problems through **hands-on engineering, experimentation, collaboration, and continuous learning**.

We believe in building robotics systems from the ground up — from **CAD models and mechanical assemblies to embedded electronics, control systems, perception, simulation, and autonomous software**.

> **Design. Build. Program. Test. Iterate.**

---

## 🤖 What We Do

### ⚙️ Mechanical Engineering

Design and develop robotic mechanisms using modern CAD and engineering analysis.

- 3D CAD modelling and assemblies
- Mechanical system design
- Mechanism development
- Structural analysis and simulation
- Manufacturing and prototyping
- Design optimization

### 💻 Robotics & Programming

Develop the software that enables our robots to perceive, decide, and act.

- C / C++ / Python
- ROS 2
- Robot simulation
- Motion and control
- Autonomous navigation
- Computer vision
- Embedded programming

### 🔌 Electronics & Control

Build the electronic and control infrastructure behind robotic systems.

- Microcontrollers
- Sensors and actuators
- Motor control
- Embedded systems
- Communication protocols
- Control-system integration

### 🏆 Competitive Robotics

We participate in robotics competitions and engineering challenges where teams are required to design and implement complete robotic systems under real constraints.

Our work involves:

**Concept → Design → Simulation → Fabrication → Integration → Testing → Competition**

## 👥 The Team

Team CiPHER is organized across four major domains:

| Domain | Focus |
|---|---|
| ⚙️ **Mechanical** | CAD, mechanisms, analysis, fabrication |
| 🔌 **Electronics** | Embedded systems, sensors, actuators, control |
| 💻 **Programming** | ROS 2, algorithms, computer vision, autonomy |
